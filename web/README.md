# Life OS: Real-Time Market Intelligence Meets Productivity

A distinctive, free-first productivity OS that intelligently integrates real-time market news, financial events, and sentiment data. Auto-generates actionable task/habit/goal recommendations when market-moving events occur.

## 🚀 Features

- **Dashboard**: Central hub with tasks, habits, goals, and live market feed
- **Market Heartbeat**: Pulse animation when breaking news arrives
- **AI Recommendations**: Groq-powered task suggestions triggered by market events
- **Real-time Alerts**: Push notifications for Fed, ECB, regulatory changes
- **Financial Goals**: Track goals with market context
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Market news sentiment (bullish/neutral/bearish)
- **Cross-border Markets**: US (Fed), EU (ECB), global tracking

## 💰 Pricing

- **Starter**: $4.99/month - 100 market alerts/day, 1 goal, basic recommendations
- **Builder**: $12.99/month - Unlimited alerts, 10 goals, real-time AI
